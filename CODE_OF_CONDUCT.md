# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AI Prison a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on the defensive governance mission and public safety.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language/imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using the project to build offensive, weaponizing or accountability-evading
  capabilities.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor
for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
